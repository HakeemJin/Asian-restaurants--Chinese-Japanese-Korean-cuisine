Taste of Asia

Exploring Chinese, Japanese & Korean Restaurants in Los Angeles

About This Map

This interactive map explores the distribution of Chinese, Japanese, and Korean restaurants across central Los Angeles. Restaurant locations and cuisine information are retrieved from OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API. Starting from Esri's Intro to Graphics sample, I customized the map's location, basemap, symbols, and popup information. Users can click restaurant markers to explore attributes and use the Layer List to toggle the three cuisine categories.
